# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is TLS?

Transport Layer Security is a protocol that encrypts network communication.

---

## What is HTTPS?

HTTP over TLS.

---

## What is a Certificate?

A digital document used to verify the identity of a server.

---

## What is OpenSSL?

An open-source toolkit for TLS and cryptography.

---

## Why does SNI matter?

It allows multiple domains to share the same IP address while presenting the correct certificate.

---

## What is a Certificate Authority?

A trusted organization that issues digital certificates.
